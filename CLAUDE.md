# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for AI/dev sessions working in this repo. Read this before touching `worker.js`.

## What this is

> **⚑ BATCH-ONLY product (Realtime + Hybrid REMOVED 2026-06-19).** Batch is the deliverable — "fast and amazingly accurate". The three-engine era (Realtime/Hybrid/direct-stream/Soniox/Deepgram) was **deleted** (worker.js 5520→3359). The full realtime investigation + a git resurrection pointer live in **`REALTIME_HANDOFF.md`** — the door is open, but this codebase is batch. There is **no engine selector** and no `sessionEngine` branching that matters (it's always `"batch"`); `loadSettings` migrates any saved engine to `"batch"`.

A one-file Cloudflare Worker. The whole product:

- **Push-to-talk → Batch.** On release, the **post-gate `MediaRecorder` webm** uploads to **ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Medical batch** (`scribe_v2_medical`, `handleTranscribeBatch`) and the accurate transcription lands on the clipboard. In batch the **noise gate is load-bearing**: the post-gate recording *is* what gets transcribed. Single-speaker medical config (`temperature=0`, `language_code=en`). **Diarization keep-primary-speaker is ON by default** (per-device `#diarize` toggle) **but applies only on the phone / big-button surface** (`diarizeActive() = #diarize && bigButtonActive()`) — a plain desktop keeps the proven single-speaker config (`num_speakers=1`/`diarize=false`) regardless of the checkbox, since flipping the default STT shape for all desktop users with no measured accuracy backing is a risk the prime directive doesn't take. On the phone surface with the toggle on, the Worker sends `diarize=true` (word-granular, no forced `num_speakers=1`) and the client (`keepPrimarySpeaker`) drops non-dominant speakers — the lever against bystander voices the mic catches, since noise suppression / iOS Voice Isolation strip *noise* but another person's speech survives them. **Guarded so it can't delete the clinician's words**: it only filters when one speaker holds ≥ `DIARIZE_PRIMARY_MIN_SHARE` (60%) of words (a roughly-even split — two real speakers, or Scribe over-diarizing one continuous voice — keeps EVERYTHING); a small removal is a clean "Done!" with a no-beep note, while a removal ≥ `DIARIZE_WARN_SHARE` (15%) is **degraded** (`warnBeep` + "verify" status + the **unfiltered transcript stashed in history** for recovery). The filter never empties a clean note (no-ops on a single speaker / missing `words[]`). When off (or on desktop), the Worker uses `diarize=false`/`num_speakers=1`.
- **Live capture feedback** (`#recFeedback`) — while recording: a scrolling voice **waveform** (canvas, driven by the analyser the gate already runs), a **"Hearing you"** indicator (gate open = speech vs silence), and an **elapsed timer**. No STT cost/latency, nothing leaves the device — it just proves the dictation is capturing. Driven from the 30 ms gate-meter loop (`showRecFeedback`/`updateRecFeedback`/`drawWave`); hidden on finalize.
- **Phone link** — dictate on the phone, the text lands on the desktop clipboard. The phone dictates **in batch** and POSTs the final text to `/api/session/<code>/deliver`; the desktop holds a listener WS to the `SessionRoom` and writes its clipboard on `phone_delivery`. (The retired realtime "live mirror" is gone; the delivery + resilience contract stays — see the Phone link section.)

Users are clinicians doing push-to-talk dictation into Cerner/Citrix via AutoHotkey, with clipboard handoff. See `README.md` for product behavior and `REALTIME_HANDOFF.md` for the (archived) realtime investigation + how to bring it back.

**Prime directive: failures must be loud.** A silent failure means wrong or missing text in a patient chart. Never trade failure visibility for a feature. An error path must never play the success beep or leave a stale clipboard unflagged.

## Status & direction

**Alpha, in real production use** (first external alpha passed June 2026). **Product = BATCH-ONLY** (see the banner at the top): batch is the deliverable, the live capture waveform is the feedback, Realtime/Hybrid were **removed** (2026-06-19) — the investigation + a resurrection path are archived in `REALTIME_HANDOFF.md`. **Go-forward focus: perfect (a) batch accuracy/latency, (b) the UI experience, and (c) the phone link.** The README's [Roadmap](README.md#roadmap) is the canonical backlog — keep it updated in the same change as the code. When priorities collide, this is the binding order:

1. **Reliability — never lose a dictation.** Loud failure is the floor, not the ceiling. The durability layers are now in place: the phone-side delivery queue (post-transcription leg) and the **IndexedDB dictation journal** (crash-safe recovery of an in-flight take — landed 2026-06-25, see Known sharp edges). Work that narrows a loss window outranks features; work that widens one — even temporarily, even behind a flag — is rejected.
2. **Settings portability.** Settings are `localStorage`-scoped: per browser profile, per device, per origin — desktop, phone, and installed PWAs don't share (iOS home-screen PWAs don't even share with Safari). The planned split: **portable** settings (keyterms, append prefs) sync across devices; **per-device** settings (gate thresholds, hotkey, mic tuning) deliberately stay local. Don't entrench new settings in ways that make that split harder — when adding one, note which side it belongs to.
3. **Mobile-first dictation UI.** ✅ Landed as the **big-button layout** (see the Phone link section): activation is the *joined state* (`joinedSessionCode`) or the per-device `bigButtonMode` override — never the screen size. The desktop compactness contract below stays unchanged; the layout is an additive fixed overlay, not a redesign.

## Constraints & style

- **No build step, no runtime dependencies, no frameworks.** The client is vanilla JS inside a template literal. Keep it that way unless explicitly asked otherwise.
- Inside `INDEX_HTML`:
  - **Never use backticks or `${`** — it's a template literal; they terminate/interpolate it. This includes comments.
  - Client-side regex/string backslashes must be **double-escaped** (`\\r\\n` in source → `\r\n` in the served page).
  - Client JS uses string concatenation, not template literals — match that.
- Settings persist under `localStorage` keys suffixed `_v9` (`scribe_v2_settings_v9`, `scribe_v2_transcripts_v9`, `elevenlabs_api_key_browser_v9`, `soniox_api_key_browser_v9` (the old realtime BYO key — now **dormant**: still read/written + the hidden `#sonioxKey` input survives, but nothing uses it since realtime was removed), `scribe_v2_passphrase_v9`, `scribe_v2_micfail_v9` (per-device capturable ring of the last `MICFAIL_LOG_MAX`=10 silent-capture diagnostics — additive, never wiped by a settings change), `scribe_v2_timing_v9` (per-device ring of the last `TIMING_LOG_MAX`=50 per-take latency breakdowns — same additive/never-wiped contract; see the Latency instrumentation section)). The **dictation journal** uses its own IndexedDB DB `scribe_v2_journal` (NOT localStorage), cleared on every finalized take. **Bumping the suffix wipes all user settings/history** — add fields to the existing schema instead; only bump on explicit request.
- `scribe_v2_access_code_v9` is the pre-merge batch app's passphrase key. `loadSettings` **must keep reading it as a fallback** (users at the legacy batch URL still have their code there); writes go to `scribe_v2_passphrase_v9`, and forget/unremember must clear both.
- **Compactness contract**: the app must stay fully usable in a tiny minimized PWA window. The primary card (record button, meter/pills, status, latest transcript — there is no engine selector anymore) stays always-visible and first in the DOM; credentials live in the Access `<details>` (auto-collapses once `hasAuth()`, reopened by `updateAuthUI` paths on missing/forgotten credentials), checkboxes + hotkey in Options, keyterms and Advanced in their own `<details>`. Put new settings inside those sections, not in always-visible rows. **The joined-mode big-button layout is additive to this**: `#bigUi` is a fixed overlay (`display:none` without `body.bigbtn`), the primary card keeps its DOM position, and nothing about the tiny-window rules changes when the layout is off — keep it that way.

## Hard invariants — do not break

- **F13 keydown starts, F14 keydown stops — always, unconditionally.** This is the AutoHotkey contract (CapsLock hold). F14 while idle also cancels a queued-but-not-yet-started session (`cancelQueuedStart`) — a session starting *after* the last F14 would violate the contract and open a mic nobody is holding. **This includes the capture tail**: while the look-ahead delay line drains, `recording` is still true *and* `stopping` is true, so the F14 branch tests `alreadyEnding = stopping || finishing` FIRST — the old `recording || stopRequested` test sent that case to `stopRecording()`, which only re-latches `stopRequested` and never reached `cancelQueuedStart()`, so an F13 queued during the tail would have started a session after the last F14 (caught by scenario 25b). The pointer (`bigBtnRelease`) and in-app hotkey release paths already guarded on `stopping || finishing || pendingStartTimer`. The configurable in-app hotkey (default Ctrl+Space; tap = toggle, hold > `HOTKEY_TAP_MS` = push-to-talk) is **additive** — it must never replace or shadow F13/F14.
- **Clipboard sentinel** is exactly `##DICTATION_FAILED##` (AHK/user workflows recognize it).
- **Beep semantics**: start/done beeps are gated by the checkbox; **failure (`failBeep`), mic-alarm (`micAlarmBeep`) and warn (`warnBeep`) sounds always play**. Beeps reuse the persistent `audioCtx` when running (a fresh `AudioContext` in a background tab starts suspended and is silent — exactly when the cue matters most). A degraded success (e.g. a joined phone whose relay-to-desktop leg is in doubt) gets `warnBeep`, never `doneBeep`.
- **Exactly one delivery per session.** The finalize path ends in exactly one `deliverFinalText()` call — one clipboard outcome, one beep. `sessionFinalized` guards finalize; the `finishing` flag spans the async upload phase and serializes sessions: F13/hotkey during it queues via `pendingStart`, never overlaps.
- **The noise gate is load-bearing**: the post-gate `MediaRecorder` recording *is* what gets uploaded and transcribed, so `MediaRecorder` construction failure is fatal (sentinel + failBeep), and an over-aggressive gate clips real speech. The gate also drives the capture-waveform feedback + the dead-mic watchdog (both read the analyser).
- **`ensureAudio` is SERIALIZED — never let two `getUserMedia` streams exist at once** (2026-09, the stuck-rebuild bug). An iOS resume fires `pageshow` + `visibilitychange` + `focus` in quick succession and each called `tryWarmOnLoad` → `ensureAudio`, so three builds ran **concurrently**. `releaseAudio()` can only stop the stream already *assigned* to the module variable, so every in-flight acquisition leaked — and two live streams on one iOS input is exactly the state where iOS feeds ONE and starves the others, so the surviving `analyserNode` read **exact zeros** while the track still reported `readyState:"live"`/unmuted and the context still read `running`. `probeMicAlive` then declared the mic dead, forced another rebuild, leaked another stream, and `#bigMicPill` stuck on "rebuilding" until an app relaunch dropped every leaked stream. The field report's own diagnostic showed the contradiction (`rs:live mute:false` beside a silence claim). Fix, and do not undo any part: (1) **coalesce** — `ensureAudio()` is a thin wrapper returning one in-flight `audioBuildPromise`, so concurrent callers join the same build; (2) **invalidate** — `releaseAudio()` bumps `audioBuildSeq`, and a build whose `getUserMedia` resolves after being superseded **stops the stream it just acquired** instead of assigning it; (3) `ensureAudio` absorbs its own "superseded" rejection with one retry, because a caller that swallows it would otherwise record with **no audio graph at all** (the press path now also fails loud on a missing graph, `precapture-no-graph`). Heal sites therefore set `audioSuspect = true` and call `tryWarmOnLoad()` **without** their own `releaseAudio()` — an extra external teardown only supersedes builds other handlers have in flight. (Scenario 47.)
- **The mic diagnostic must never report a stale peak.** Every pre-capture failure (mic unavailable, the corpse probe, no graph) prints `micDiag` and returns *before* the per-take reset, so `peak:` was the **previous** take's level — a "the mic is delivering silence" headline printed beside a healthy `peak:0.12579`, which sent this very bug's diagnosis in the wrong direction. `startRecording` now zeroes `maxRmsSeen`/`lastProbeRms` up front, and `micDiag` adds **`probe:`**, the reading the corpse probe itself just took. (Scenario 47.)
- **Shared mode**: the master API key must never reach the browser; the Worker injects it server-side into the ElevenLabs batch request after the constant-time passphrase check (`safeEqual`) on the `POST /api/transcribe` path. (The phone-link session routes are gated by the same passphrase — the WS upgrade carries it as `?auth=`, fetches as the `x-phone-auth` header; the master key never rides the link — see the Phone link section + Known sharp edges.)
- **A failed upload must NEVER discard the recording** (2026-09). `finishBatchSession`'s `!r.ok` branch calls `armUploadRecovery(blob)` and passes `keepJournal`/`recoverable` into `deliverFinalText`, which then calls `journalDetach()` (null the id, **keep** the IndexedDB record) instead of `journalFinish()` (delete it). Clearing it there was a real data-loss bug: a timed-out take lost the audio along with the text and the only option was to redictate from memory. The outcome stays **loud** (sentinel + `failBeep` + red status) and the recovery is purely additive: the status adds "Your RECORDING was saved — use 'Retry the upload'", the existing `#journalRecover` banner is reused with upload wording (`showUploadRecover`), and because the `#bigUi` overlay covers that card, the phone gets `#bigRecoverChip` (`updateRecoverChip`) as the tappable equivalent. `batchTranscribe` returns `retryable`: only a recording the service can never accept (413, or a 400 naming too-short/too-large/empty) is permanent and offers no retry — a 5xx, a 429 and even a wrong access code all keep the audio. A **retry on a joined phone relays to the desktop** through the normal queue+ack path (`recoverPendingDictation`), since the desktop clipboard is the deliverable there. `armUploadRecovery` is deliberately **silent** — `deliverFinalText` owns the single outcome beep. (Scenario 46.)
- **The streamed upload can only make a take faster — never change or lose it** (2026-09, ported from WhisperInk's `StreamedTranscription`). On Chrome/Edge (`uploadStreamingSupported()`: fetch upload streaming needs HTTP/2; Safari/Firefox/jsdom never stream) with the per-device `#streamUpload` toggle on (additive `_v9` settings field `streamUpload`, default on — per-device because whether a network can stream is a property of the machine, not the clinician), `startBatchRecording` opens `POST /api/transcribe-stream` (`openTakeStream`) and every recorder chunk goes up as it is produced (`takeStreamAppend`, in the same `ondataavailable` that feeds `chunks[]` and the journal — the stream is a copy, never the source). The Worker (`handleTranscribeStream`) opens the ElevenLabs request at the first frame and relays each chunk into the ordinary multipart request (the fields come from the SAME `sttUpstreamFields` as the batch proxy; the file part goes last), so the release waits only for the transcript. The rules, do not weaken any: (1) **byte + chunk check before the body ends, on both sides** — `finishBatchSession` claims the stream in the same synchronous step that assembles the blob (`claimTakeStream`), `transcribeViaStream` compares bytes/chunks with the blob before sending the end frame, and the Worker writes the multipart closing boundary ONLY after the end frame matches what it relayed; anything else **aborts** the upstream body so ElevenLabs never receives a complete request (nothing transcribed or billed). (2) **Any failure except the deadline falls back** to `batchTranscribe` of the in-memory blob under what is left of the same deadline (`transcribeTake`) — that path owns the error semantics, so streaming changes speed, never outcomes; a refusal (any non-2xx) falls back too. (3) **The deadline is final** on the stream path exactly as on the ordinary one: loud (sentinel + failBeep), recording kept for a retry, and NO second upload (it would double the wait past `hotkey.ahk`'s `CLIP_TIMEOUT`). (4) A take that ends without an upload (blob < 1 KB) **cancels** the stream mid-body. (5) `STREAM_FAIL_DISABLE` (2) consecutive fallbacks/timeouts switch streaming off until reload (`streamOffReason`), so a network that cannot stream costs at most two slower takes; past `STREAM_MAX_REC_MS` (5 min) a take's stream is given up and it uploads the ordinary way. Credentials ride headers (`x-app-auth` shared / `x-el-key` BYO), never the URL. The timing ring records `path` (`stream` / `batch` / `stream>batch`) + `pathNote` (why). Measured in WhisperInk on `scribe_v2_medical`: 4 s take 240 vs 323 ms, 11 s take 307 vs 402 ms; the web port additionally skips the Worker→ElevenLabs connection setup at release. (Scenarios 50, 51.)
- **Failure-aware finalize**: an upload failure / no-text outcome delivers loud — red status + fail beep, sentinel on the clipboard when there's no text. A failed clipboard write must fail-beep — with one scoped exception: on a **joined** device with an otherwise-clean outcome, the local copy is best-effort (iOS denies clipboard writes outside a user gesture, and the **desktop** clipboard is the deliverable there) — the outcome cue defers to the relay ack via `announceRelayOutcome`/`relayDeliveryToDesktop(text, announceOutcome, degradedNote)`: done beep on a listener ack, **warn beep + VERIFY on a listener ack carrying degraded content** (a big speaker-filter cut or a coverage shortfall — a degraded outcome never gets the done beep, even delivered), red warn/fail on zero-listeners or relay failure. Still exactly one outcome beep per session; unexpected/mic-alarm outcomes keep the loud local copy-failure path even when joined.
- **Transcript-coverage guard** (`coverageShortfall`, in `finishBatchSession`): every dictation requests **word timestamps** (`timestamps_granularity=word`, always — the old Advanced select is retired/hidden), and a result whose last word ends more than max(`COVERAGE_MIN_SLACK_S`, `COVERAGE_SLACK_FRAC`×take) short of the observed speech — or whose decoded `audio_duration_secs` is that much shorter than the recording — is **degraded**: "⚠ Transcript may be INCOMPLETE — covered M:SS of M:SS" + warn beep, text still delivered. The baseline is the **last gate-open moment** (tracked per 30 ms tick as `lastGateOpenAtMs`, with `gateOpenMs`/`deadBandMs` accumulated alongside), NOT the wall-clock hold — a clinician who keeps the button pressed after finishing must never be false-warned. Missing words/timestamps/duration ⇒ clean no-op. Every shortfall logs a `coverage-shortfall` entry (rec/speechUntil/lastWordEnd/audioDur/gateOpen/deadBand/blob/chunks) to the micfail ring: a large `deadBand` tail fingers gate-drift (the recording itself lacks the audio — tune gain/gate), gate-open-to-the-end fingers service truncation.
- `cleanTranscript` semantics — strip ellipsis pause markers (Scribe renders pauses as "…"/"..."), strip newlines, collapse spaces, tighten space-before-punctuation, append a trailing space — downstream paste workflows depend on them. **These are now unconditional**: the four toggles (Remove ellipses / Strip newlines / Trailing space / Remove filler words=`no_verbatim`) were removed from the UI and hardcoded on (their old defaults), since they're the only sane shape for paste-into-Cerner. Their `_v9` fields are no longer written/read (old saved values are ignored).

## Client session state machine

One session per dictation, guarded by `sessionSeq` (stale callbacks bail out), `sessionFinalized` (finalize runs exactly once), and `finishing` (delivery still in flight; new sessions queue).

```
idle
 └─ startRecording(): credential check (ElevenLabs key / passphrase), ensureAudio(),
    acquireWakeLock(), per-session resets, precompute batch keyterms + TLS pre-warm,
    append decision (appendArmed one-shot, else the append-mode checkbox —
    no time window), sessionBaseText snapshot
     └─ startBatchRecording(): post-gate MediaRecorder.start(1000) IS the capture
        path (the #recFeedback waveform/timer runs off the analyser meanwhile);
        openTakeStream() starts the streamed upload (Chrome/Edge, best-effort)
         └─ stopRecording() [PTT release]: mediaRecorder.stop()
             └─ recorder.onstop → finalizeSession(false) → finishBatchSession():
                claimTakeStream() → transcribeTake(): the streamed transcript when
                the stream held, else upload webm → ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Medical batch
                (BATCH_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS, duration/size-aware)
                → splice onto sessionBaseText → deliverFinalText
```

- **`deliverFinalText` is the single delivery exit** — exactly one clipboard outcome + one beep per session. No text ⇒ sentinel + failBeep; clean ⇒ copy + doneBeep; unexpected/mic-alarm ⇒ copy + warn/fail. Joined (phone) ⇒ also `relayDeliveryToDesktop`, and a denied local copy on an otherwise-clean outcome defers to the relay ack (see Phone link).
- Any unexpected failure ⇒ `finalizeSession(true)` / `deliverFinalText({unexpected:true})` ⇒ red status + fail beep + sentinel-if-no-text.
- A 30 ms watchdog (inside the gate meter loop) fires the mic alarm on dead/muted track or RMS flatline (< `FLATLINE_RMS` after 2.5 s) — works in batch (no WS dependency).
- F13 during finalization or delivery sets `pendingStart`; `maybePendingStart()` starts the next session after `deliverFinalText` — via a cancellable `pendingStartTimer` (60 ms; **1.5 s after a failure outcome** so the red screen/status is seen before the next REC paints over it). On the phone-link path the queued start additionally waits for the relay ack (`relayDeliveryToDesktop(...).finally(maybePendingStart)`, deadline `RELAY_TIMEOUT_MS`). **A queued start dies when the press that queued it ends without a tap**: a hold released during the finalize/queued window, a cancelled pointer, or F14 calls `cancelQueuedStart()` — the deferred `startRecording` must never open a mic nobody is holding. `finishing` stays true through the delivery's status/beep branches (cleared just before the relay/queue tail), so the big-screen derivation shows WORKING… across the awaits instead of a stale state.
- **Append-next + box editing**: the **"➕ Append next"** button (`toggleAppendArm`) arms `appendArmed` — a one-shot "append the next dictation" regardless of the append-mode checkbox (off by default); consumed at session start, cleared whenever the box empties, ignored mid-session. **There is no time window** — append vs. fresh is purely the one-shot arm or the checkbox state, never clock-dependent (the old `appendWindow` setting + `lastFinalizeAt` were removed 2026-06-22 because the timed cliff silently wiped notes). The chip + box border + the button's `active` state surface it. **Clicking/focusing the `#latest` box also arms append** (its `focus` handler — idle + non-empty, mirroring `toggleAppendArm`): the box already lights up on `:focus` with the same accent glow as `.armed`, so click-to-edit and click-to-append are one gesture; the caret is placed normally (no select-all — a stray keystroke must never wipe the note), and the button is the explicit toggle/cancel. (The big-peek expanded text also forwards taps to `toggleAppendArm`.) **The transcript box itself is now hand-editable** (`#latest` is `contenteditable` while idle — `refreshLatestEditable()` derives editability from session state so the live/finalize path owns the text mid-session): typing rewrites `latestText`/`finalizedSegments` (no `cleanTranscript` mid-type — delivery still cleans), so Copy/Append/delivery all use the edited text, and a `paste` handler (`plainTextPaste`) forces plain text. **Every transcript surface is directly hand-editable in place** (2026-06-23) — the active box, the `#lastDictation` slot, and **History rows** all share one model: `contenteditable` while idle, click-to-place-caret, `plainTextPaste`, persist on `blur`, no Edit/Save/Cancel buttons. **History rows** (`renderHistory`): editing persists keyed by `createdAt` (a re-render can't hit the wrong row) and stamps the additive `editedAt` ("· edited" in the meta, updated *in place* — the blur handler writes `localStorage` directly rather than `setHistory`, so the live re-render can't tear down the row you're editing); `Escape` reverts to the pre-edit text. The **slot** (`#lastDictationText`) persists its edit back to the matching history entry on blur (matched by the pre-edit text via `slotEditBase`; `input` keeps `archivedText` live so the slot's Copy/Append use the edited text; a blanked slot never wipes the saved note). (Scenario 31, 32.)
- **Copy-files-and-readies + the "Last dictation" slot** (Option B, 2026-06-23): the `#latest` box is the *active* note and holds the just-finished dictation until it's explicitly **filed** — at which point it drops into the **`#lastDictation` slot** (under the box, above History) and the box clears for a fresh note. Filing happens on **Copy & clear** (`copyBtn` — copy to clipboard, then `fileToSlot` + `clearBox`; a *failed* copy keeps the box so nothing is lost before the deliverable landed), on **Clear dictation box** (`freshBtn`), and at **fresh session start** (the non-append branch files `finalizedSegments` before clearing). `deliverFinalText` does **not** file — the box keeps the delivered note so it stays editable/appendable until the clinician is done with it. The slot's state is `archivedText` (in-memory; re-derived from `history[1]` at boot by `restoreLatestFromHistory`); `fileToSlot` no-ops on empty text so filing an already-empty box never wipes the slot. `renderLastDictation()` (called from `updateLiveDisplay`/`updateAppendChip`) **stays visible & editable at any time** — it is hidden *only* when there is genuinely nothing filed and it isn't being edited (an empty slot can't persist an edit anyway, since there's no history entry to match); it no longer disappears mid-session (2026-06-24). It never rewrites `textContent` while the slot is focused (would collapse the caret mid-edit). **Caret fix (2026-06-24)**: the slot keeps a 96px display cap (`max-height`/`overflow:auto`), but on `:focus` the cap is lifted (`max-height:none; overflow:visible`) so the caret is never clipped by the overflow container while editing/adding a new line. Slot buttons: **Copy** (`lastCopyBtn` — re-grab the filed note, no box change) and **➕ Append to this** (`lastAppendBtn` — load the filed note back into the box + arm `appendArmed`; *disabled when the box is non-empty or mid-session* so it can never clobber a different active note or fight a live session — append always targets what's in the box). Clearing history (`clearBtn`) empties `archivedText` too. **Desktop/normal layout only** — the slot lives in the primary card, which the `#bigUi` overlay covers, so the phone big-button surface is unchanged. (Scenario 32.)
- **Boot restore**: `restoreLatestFromHistory()` puts the newest history entry into the box (and `history[1]` into the `#lastDictation` slot) so the note stays visible across reloads; click-to-append (or append mode) can then extend it.
- Mic re-engagement: `audioGraphHealthy()` checks the actual `MediaStreamTrack.readyState` **and `muted`** (iOS interruptions — lock screen, Siri, calls — leave the track "live" but permanently muted), not just variable presence; rebuilt on start and on `pageshow` / `visibilitychange` / `focus` (standalone PWAs can fire only focus on app switch) / `devicechange`, and an idle track that stays muted > 1.2 s self-heals. bfcache restores leave dead streams that *look* alive. **The resume silent-capture bug** (the worst real-world failure: joined PWA, resume the session, the mic *looks* engaged but records nothing): iOS leaves a track that died while the PWA was backgrounded reporting `readyState:"live"`/unmuted with **no `ended` event**, so `audioGraphHealthy()` trusts a corpse and `ensureAudio()` reuses a dead graph. Fix: **any backgrounding** (`pagehide` and `visibilitychange`→hidden) sets `audioSuspect`, which makes `ensureAudio()` **skip the healthy-reuse fast path and force a fresh `getUserMedia`** (a fresh track is genuinely live; the flag is cleared on rebuild); the foreground handlers also re-warm on `audioSuspect`, so the rebuild usually happens proactively and the press stays fast. **AudioContext-state alarm**: a suspended/`interrupted` context mid-dictation freezes the `AnalyserNode` (it returns the *same stale buffer*), so the RMS-flatline watchdog goes blind on stale non-zero peaks — the watchdog therefore also alarms on `audioCtx.state !== "running"` (recording-only, after a >200 ms start-up grace) — but **debounced**: it must stay non-running for `CTX_INTERRUPT_GRACE_MS` (400 ms, tracked via `ctxNotRunningSince`) before firing, because iOS emits spurious `interrupted`→`running` blips that drop no audio and the original instant-fire turned those healthy takes into failed dictations (the mic-stability regression, fixed 2026-06-24). A **sustained** interruption (audio actually lost, analyser frozen) still alarms within ~half a second and is **never auto-recovered** (fail loud, redictate). The `audioCtx.onstatechange` listener no longer fires instantly either: it stamps `ctxNotRunningSince` and arms a deferred `maybeAlarmCtxInterrupted` re-check after the grace (the backstop for when the 30 ms gate timer is throttled). iOS Safari has no Permissions API entry for the mic, so `tryWarmOnLoad` falls back to `micEverGranted` — **persisted as the additive `micGranted` settings field**, so a killed-and-relaunched PWA re-warms at boot instead of staying cold. Re-warms go through `warmWithRetry` (widening backoff ~700 ms → 5 s, ~5 attempts): iOS hands the audio session back late after foregrounding (longer after a call/Siri/a long lock), so the first `getUserMedia` can fail and succeed moments later; after the retries it gives up with a visible warn status. **Screen wake lock — `wakeLockDesired()` decides:** held from session start to `deliverFinalText` so iOS auto-lock cannot reclaim the mic mid-dictation or suspend the page mid-upload, AND held the **whole time the phone sits on the big-button surface** (`bigButtonActive()`) — acquired in `applyBigButtonUI`, *not* released after a delivery there, re-acquired on `pageshow`/`visibilitychange`/`focus` (the OS drops the lock on hide), released only on Leave. This is the load-bearing fix for "iOS keeps killing the mic between takes": keeping the screen awake prevents the auto-lock interruption instead of only re-warming after it. `acquireWakeLock()` is idempotent (won't stack sentinels) and now **re-acquires on the sentinel's `release` event** (gated on `bigButtonActive()` + visible) — iOS drops the lock on its own (low-power mode, system events) with no visibility event to trigger the existing re-arm, so the screen would sleep and reclaim the mic between takes; the gate on `bigButtonActive` (not the broader `wakeLockDesired`) keeps an intentional desktop release at `deliverFinalText` — fired while `finishing` is still true — from being instantly undone. **Press-path `getUserMedia` retry (2026-06-24):** because `audioSuspect` forces a fresh `getUserMedia` after any backgrounding, the FIRST press back in the app raced iOS's late audio-session hand-back and died with "Microphone unavailable" (the "mic won't engage after returning" regression); `startRecording` now retries `ensureAudio()` up to 3× on a ~350 ms gap before the loud failure, failing fast only on a real permission denial (`NotAllowedError`/`SecurityError`). **Between-takes corpse-mic guard (2026-06-24):** on the big-button surface the audio graph is *retained* between takes for a fast next PTT, but iOS can silently kill the mic track in the idle gap while it keeps reporting `readyState:"live"`/unmuted — `audioGraphHealthy()` trusts the corpse and the next take records pure silence (the field "MIC NOT CAPTURING — no audio signal detected" flatline, with the OS mic indicator still lit). The wake lock tries to *prevent* the reclaim but isn't sufficient alone, so `finalizeSession` sets `audioSuspect` whenever `bigButtonActive()`: the next `ensureAudio()` rebuilds from a fresh `getUserMedia` (genuinely live) and the press-path retry covers iOS's late hand-back. Desktop reuse stays fast (no iOS corpse-mic gap). (Scenario 25b.) On a plain desktop the lifecycle is unchanged (released at `deliverFinalText`).

## Phone link (dictate on the phone, clipboard on the desktop)

One Durable Object room per 6-char session code (`SessionRoom`, top of `worker.js`; route `/api/session/<code>`). The desktop holds a listener WebSocket to the room; the phone joins by code, dictates **in batch** locally, and its `deliverFinalText` POSTs the authoritative final text to `/api/session/<code>/deliver`. The desktop's `phone_delivery` handler is what writes the desktop clipboard. (The realtime "live mirror" — the phone streaming transcript frames into the room for on-the-desktop preview — was removed with the realtime engine; the desktop now sees text only on the final `phone_delivery`, never word-by-word.)

**The desktop OWNS append in the phone-link flow (2026-06-24).** A joined phone is a *dumb mic*: `startRecording` forces a fresh single dictation when `joinedSessionCode` is set (append-mode/arm ignored, click-to-append suppressed in `toggleAppendArm` + the box-focus arm), so it always delivers ONE segment. The **desktop** then appends or replaces in `deliverRemoteText` per **this device's** append mode / one-shot arm — mirroring single-desktop append, which a clinician expects to apply no matter which mic dictated. This single-owner split is deliberate: if both sides appended you'd get **double-append = wrong text on a chart**. The desktop's `delivery_id` dedupe ring runs *before* the append, so a replayed/retried delivery can never double-append. (A *solo* big-button device — `bigButtonMode:"always"`, not joined — still appends locally; only a real join hands ownership to the desktop.) Scenario 20 (desktop appends) + 25b (joined phone delivers single).

Resilience contract — every layer of this link fails silently by default; do not weaken these:

- **Access-code gate + rate caps (2026-07)**: the 6-char code *routes* to a room; it must not also be the only thing *guarding* it (an unauthenticated `/deliver` could plant attacker text on a clinician's clipboard; `/latest` would hand the held note to anyone who guessed a live code). When the worker has `APP_PASSPHRASE` set, **every `/api/session` route requires it** — the `x-phone-auth` header on fetches, `?auth=` on the WS upgrade (browsers can't set WS headers) — checked with the constant-time `safeEqual` **before any DO is instantiated**, so a code scan can't read/forge notes, use `/status` as a live-code oracle, or run up DO costs. A deploy without `APP_PASSPHRASE` (BYO self-host) keeps code-only behavior. Rate limiting is **two layers**, both per-IP and both BEFORE the DO: (1) the **global** Cloudflare-native Workers Rate Limiting binding (`env.SESSION_RL` / `env.SESSION_FAIL_RL`, provisioned in `wrangler.toml`), whose counters aggregate across every isolate in a Cloudflare location so they bound a **distributed** scan the per-isolate Map can't — **feature-detected + FAIL-OPEN** (an absent binding or a limiter error never 500s a delivery); (2) the **per-isolate** Map backstop (`LINK_RL_*`: 600 total/min sized for clinic NATs; 20 failed-auth/min consulted **only** on failed auth so a stale client can never throttle an authed neighbor) that still works when the binding is absent (BYO self-host). Both consult the same fail budget only on failed auth. Plus a 100 KB `/deliver` body cap (`DELIVER_MAX_BYTES` — the ring persists to a single DO storage value). (A classic dashboard WAF Rate Limiting Rule needs a zone; this Worker is on `*.workers.dev`, so the in-repo binding is the equivalent global layer.) **Client side, a 401 is always loud and never costs text**: the delivery stays queued and the failure cue names the fix ("requires the ACCESS CODE"), the `/latest`/`/status` polls surface a one-shot red status (the WS can't distinguish a 401 from a drop — the poll is the desktop's diagnoser), and starting/joining the link with no code saved warns at setup time (`linkAuthSetupWarning`). The AHK poller sends the header via `PHONE_AUTH`. (Scenario 42.)
- **Heartbeat + reconnect**: the desktop pings the room every `PHONE_PING_INTERVAL_MS`; no room traffic for `PHONE_PONG_TIMEOUT_MS` (sized for background-tab timer throttling) = zombie socket — force-close and reconnect with backoff (cap `PHONE_RECONNECT_MAX_MS`) for as long as `phoneSessionCode` is set. A drop flips the code badge to `⚠`/danger with a red status. The badge being visible is **not** proof of a live link — only the heartbeat is. **A healed link CLEARS the red (2026-09):** `schedulePhoneReconnect` sets `phoneLinkDropped`, and `ws.onopen` on the reconnect replaces the alarm with a neutral "Phone link restored — code X is live again." (never `ok` — a healed link is not a delivered dictation). Before this the badge lost its ⚠ but the status, window wash and title stayed FAILED until an unrelated status overwrote them — a false red beside a working link, which is how users learn to ignore red. While the flag is up and no take is in flight, `applyWindowState` titles the tab "⚠ LINK DOWN — Dictation" instead of "⚠ FAILED" so a dropped link cannot be mistaken for a lost take. (Scenario 26L.) **Foreground reconnect (`reconnectPhoneLinkIfNeeded`, 2026-06-24):** background tabs get frozen and their heartbeat/backoff timers throttled, so a listener socket that died while the desktop sat behind Cerner/Citrix would stay dead — and a phone delivery sent meanwhile would miss the replay window — until a throttled tick finally fired (the "deliveries don't land after backgrounding" regression). So `visibilitychange`→visible and `focus` now revive the link *immediately*: an open socket is just pinged (`phoneHeartbeat` decides zombie-or-not), a closed/null one reconnects at once (cancelling the frozen backoff timer, resetting the delay). The room replays the buffered delivery on reconnect, so the note lands the moment the clinician looks at the desktop. This strictly **narrows** the loss window — it never force-closes a healthy socket. No-op on the phone (it has `joinedSessionCode`, not `phoneSessionCode`). **`/latest` poll fallback (`pollLatestDeliveries`, 2026-07):** the WS is still the primary path, but while it is not OPEN a `POLL_LATEST_MS` interval (armed in `beginPhoneSession`, cleared in `stopPhoneSession`; hidden tabs throttle it to ~1/min — still lands a note within a minute instead of never) sweeps the room's fresh `deliveries` ring through the NORMAL `handlePhoneSessionMessage` path — the persisted dedupe ring and desktop-owns-append apply identically, so a polled delivery is indistinguishable from a WS one. The foreground handlers also fire one **forced** sweep past an OPEN-looking socket (a zombie can read OPEN for up to the pong timeout; the sweep is dedupe-safe, so checking is free). Falls back to the old single-`delivery` shape against an old worker. Best-effort — the WS reconnect machinery stays the loud path.
- **Buffered delivery + dedupe**: the room retains recent `phone_delivery`s in a **ring persisted to DO storage** (≤ `DELIVERY_RETAIN_MAX` = 5, retention `DELIVERY_RETAIN_MS` = 30 min — survives room eviction; the old single in-memory 2-min slot silently lost undelivered notes on idle reclaim) and **replays every fresh entry** to (re)connecting listeners; a storage **`alarm()` prunes** expired text so it never lingers server-side past retention (`ensureLoaded`/`persistDeliveries` are fully try/caught — a storage failure degrades to the in-memory behavior, never a 500). The room also answers **`GET /status`** (`{listeners, buffered}` — the phone presence pill's cue, never load-bearing). The phone stamps each delivery with a `delivery_id` and the desktop dedupes against a **ring of recent ids** (`recentDeliveryIds`, additive `_v9`; the legacy single `lastDeliveryId` is migrated in), so replays — **and the phone-side queue's retried/out-of-order re-POSTs** — can never double-copy. The dedupe ring (12) deliberately exceeds the room ring (5): replaying the whole room ring can never re-copy. The 30-min retention deliberately matches `DELIVERY_QUEUE_TTL_MS` — the phone auto-re-POSTs a note that old anyway; the room path just stopped being the weak link.
- **Phone-side delivery queue** (durability — the joined device): `relayDeliveryToDesktop` **enqueues the text first** (persisted as `pendingDeliveries`, additive `_v9`, **per-device**), then flushes FIFO. A POST that fails or acks **zero listeners** keeps the item queued and **retried** — on link heal (`online`/`visibilitychange`/`focus`, a backoff timer `DELIVERY_QUEUE_RETRY_MS`→`…_MAX_MS`) and **at boot** (`restorePhoneLink` → `backgroundFlush`, so a phone that died after transcribing but before delivering recovers on relaunch) — until a listener acks (then it's dropped). Flushes are serialized through `flushChain` so re-POSTs never interleave (FIFO room ordering), and each retry reuses the **same `delivery_id`** (the desktop ring dedupes the double). The flush returns the current dictation's outcome so finalize still emits **exactly one** cue (done on a listener ack incl. the deferred joined-copy-denied path, warn on zero-listeners, fail on a dead POST). Bounded by `DELIVERY_QUEUE_MAX` + a `DELIVERY_QUEUE_TTL_MS` staleness drop (a too-old note never auto-pastes hours later; it stays in history). **Items are CODE-BOUND** (`item.code`, stamped at enqueue; legacy items get the persisted join stamped at load): the flush **skips** items bound to a different code — they can never auto-POST to the wrong desktop (the wrong-chart safety) — and **Leave / joining a different code KEEP the queue** (the old wipes destroyed undelivered patient text when a clinician re-linked, because re-linking passes through Leave — the 2026-07 field incident). Rejoining the same code **auto-flushes immediately** (the join handler calls `backgroundFlush`), so the stranded-note incident self-heals; foreign-code items age out via the TTL or go via the manual send. The **queue chip** (`updateQueueChip` → `#queueChip` on the primary card + `#bigQueueChip` on the big screen, hidden when empty) makes undelivered notes visible — "N waiting to send — tap to retry" — and a tap flushes now (`queueChipTapped`). This strictly *narrows* the loss window — never widens it. (Scenario 20q, 34.)
- **Manual "Send to desktop"** (`sendTextToDesktop`): joined + **idle-only** (never mid-session — its cue must not collide with the one-outcome-beep invariant), pushes EXISTING text through the normal `relayDeliveryToDesktop` queue+cue path with a **fresh `delivery_id`** (an explicit re-send SHOULD deliver again; the desktop's append mode applies as with any delivery). Surfaces: `#sendDesktopBtn` in the box's button row (visible only when joined), a per-row "📤 Send to desktop" button on history rows (added in `renderHistory` when joined), and `#bigSendBtn` in the expanded big-peek (the overlay covers the card, so that's the phone surface). This is the universal recovery for a stranded note — including one past the queue TTL or bound to an old code (history keeps the text). (Scenario 34.)
- **Desktop-presence pill (phone)**: the phone holds no socket, so it used to learn the desktop was gone only AFTER a dictation's zero-listener ack. On the big-button surface a joined phone polls **`GET /status`** every `STATUS_POLL_MS` while visible (+ immediately on join/foreground; armed/cleared in `applyBigButtonUI`) and drives `#bigDesktopPill` — "🖥 Desktop ✓" / "🖥 Desktop not listening" — so the clinician can see the link state BEFORE speaking. **A cue, never a gate** (recording proceeds regardless; the queue catches an offline desktop), hidden on any fetch failure or an old worker's 404 so it can never mislead. A positive poll with queued notes triggers `backgroundFlush` immediately — the "desktop just came back" heal skips the retry backoff. **The CHANGE is also said in words (2026-09):** `setDesktopPill` compares against the previous `desktopPresent` and, idle-only (never mid-take) and only on a real transition, sets a **warn** status when the desktop goes away ("No desktop is listening for code X — it may have ended the session… notes queue here… or tap Leave desktop") and a neutral one when it returns. The pill alone left the status line reading "Paired … hold the button to dictate" under a red "not listening", with no next step. A steady state never re-announces. Still a cue, never a gate. (Scenario 36.)
- **Delivery ack**: `/deliver` answers with the room's listener count. **Whether zero listeners is a FAILURE depends on whether a desktop was expected** (2026-09). The phone is a complete dictation device on its own — the take is transcribed, in the box, in history, and in the durable code-bound queue that sends itself when the desktop returns — so a zero-listener ack on a phone that already knew no desktop was there is the phone-only workflow **working**, and it gets `doneBeep` + an `ok` status that says plainly it is QUEUED and has NOT reached the desktop. Reporting those as failures meant every take on a phone-only shift painted the screen FAILED, which is how a loud-failure product teaches its user to ignore red. The loud path (red + `warnBeep`) is kept for the genuine surprise: a desktop that WAS listening (or whose presence was never established) and did not receive the note — and it fires on the CHANGE only, since the ack updates `desktopPresent`, so the next take is the quiet expected case. `desktopKnownAtStart` snapshots `desktopPresent` at `startRecording`, and `setDesktopPill` (the `/status` poll) plus every ack keep that memory fresh. A dead POST stays `failBeep` regardless. Never restore fire-and-forget here, and never let the cue claim the desktop has the text when it does not. (Scenario 48.)
- **The phone's local copy is best-effort while joined**: iOS denies clipboard writes outside a user gesture, and by delivery time (post-upload) there is none — so on a joined device a denied local copy on an otherwise-clean outcome must NOT read as a failure (the desktop clipboard is the deliverable). The outcome cue defers to the relay ack ("Delivered to the desktop clipboard. Done!" + done beep on a listener ack; the existing red paths on zero-listeners/relay failure). Unjoined, a denied copy stays the loud failure it always was.
- **Focus-retry copy**: a delivery whose clipboard write fails (tab unfocused behind Citrix/Cerner) is held in `pendingCopyText` with red status + fail beep, and retried on the window `focus` event. This is the sanctioned exception to "don't silently retry clipboard writes later" — it is not silent; the status stays red until the retry lands.
- **`phone_session_end` / live-mirror fallback is now vestigial**: it existed because the phone's realtime socket closed *before* the batch refine finished, so the desktop kept a `PHONE_FALLBACK_GRACE_MS` grace timer and the accumulated live `remoteCommitted` text as a degraded fallback. With the realtime mirror removed, nothing emits `phone_session_end` and `remoteCommitted` never fills — the desktop relies entirely on the authoritative `phone_delivery` POST (acked + replayed + deduped). The handlers are dormant/harmless. The phone-that-dies-before-delivering gap is now closed by the **phone-side delivery queue** (see above): an undelivered relay is persisted and retried on heal/boot.
- **Audible desktop cues**: the desktop listener never records, so `audioCtx` may not exist; `beepCtx` is warmed from the session-start click (a user gesture) and `beep()` falls back to it. Without this, every fail beep on the listener is silent. (A boot-time session resume has no gesture — `restorePhoneLink` arms a one-shot warm-up on the first pointerdown/keydown.)
- **Front-and-center pairing — BOTH sides named (2026-09)**: the same page serves both ends of the link, so the primary card carries **two** buttons side by side: **"📱 Pair a phone"** (what the DESKTOP clicks — show a code) and **"🖥 Pair to a desktop"** (what the PHONE taps — type the desktop's code). A user on the phone used to see only "Pair a phone" and read it backwards. **Phone side:** `#pairDesktopBtn` opens `#joinOverlay` (`openJoinOverlay`/`closeJoinOverlay`) — a code input whose Join goes through **`joinDesktopSession(code)`**, the ONE join path shared with the Options "Join desktop" row (which mirrors a join made anywhere); reopened while joined it names the code and offers "Leave this desktop" via **`leaveDesktopSession()`**, the ONE leave path (the Options Leave, the big-button Leave and the overlay all call it). The button reads "🖥 Paired to desktop CODE ✓" while joined (`updatePairButton` owns both buttons' text). **The big-button top row carries `#bigPairBtn` "🖥 Pair to a desktop"** whenever `!joinedSessionCode` (a solo phone on the "always" override had NO way to reach a desktop — the overlay covers the card), hidden once joined (Leave takes its place); the badge reads "Paired to desktop CODE" / "Not paired — notes stay on this device". `#joinOverlay` is deliberately **not** hidden under `body.bigbtn` (z-index 52, above `#bigUi`), unlike `#pairOverlay`. (Scenario 26d/26s.) **Desktop side:** "📱 Pair a phone" opens `#pairOverlay` — a fixed, centered **big-QR** overlay (`openPairOverlay`/`closePairOverlay`) that auto-closes the moment a phone joins. **Additive to the compactness contract**: the QR is big *only while pairing*; the always-visible footprint is just one button, and the overlay is `display:none` until shown (and force-hidden under `body.bigbtn`). Join detection uses a **`phone_join` handshake**: the phone POSTs `{message_type:"phone_join"}` through the existing `/deliver`→`/broadcast` path (the room relays it to listeners and does **not** buffer it — only `phone_delivery` is buffered), the desktop's `onPhoneJoined` closes the overlay + flips the button to "Phone paired ✓", and the **first `phone_delivery` is the fallback** if the (unbuffered) ping was missed. `phoneJoined` is desktop-only session state (reset on start/stop), not persisted.
- **Phone-recording indicator (desktop)**: so the desktop user can see audio is being captured *before* the text lands, the joined phone fires a **`phone_recording` ping** (`{message_type:"phone_recording", state:"start"|"stop"}`, `notifyDesktopRecording`) through the same `/deliver`→`/broadcast` relay — **unbuffered**, fire-and-forget, gated on a join, exactly like `phone_join`. `startBatchRecording` sends `"start"`, `finalizeSession` sends `"stop"`. The desktop's `setPhoneRecIndicator` drives a live `#phoneRecBadge` on the primary card (pulsing red dot "Phone is recording…" → muted "Phone finished — transcribing…") plus a status line, and `phone_delivery` clears it. **The indicator is a cue, never load-bearing**: a missed ping just misses the cue (a `phone_recording` also counts as proof a phone is on the link, calling `onPhoneJoined`), and a safety timer auto-clears it (long for recording, `BATCH_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS`+margin for transcribing) if a stop/delivery is missed. Desktop-only (gated `!joinedSessionCode`), reset on session end. (Scenario 26r.) **`phone_leave` (2026-09):** `leaveDesktopSession` fires `notifyDesktopOfLeave(code)` through the same unbuffered relay *before* clearing the join; the desktop handler drops `phoneJoined`, clears the recording indicator, and sets a **warn** status ("The phone left the session. Code X stays open — pair the phone again with the same code") — the paired cue used to outlive the phone, so a clinician would dictate into a link with no mic on it. A repeat/unpaired `phone_leave` is a silent no-op. The desktop's "📱 Pair a phone" button has three states: "Pair a phone" (no session) / "Waiting for phone · CODE" (session running, no phone yet — a phone user who tapped it by mistake can now SEE it running and reopen → End session) / "Phone paired ✓". The overlay's hide button reads "Hide (keep waiting)", not "Done", because the session keeps listening. (Scenario 26L.)
- **Mic-tips onboarding (keep other voices out)**: bystander speech in a note is the "silent wrong text" failure, and **iOS Voice Isolation** (Control Center → Mic Mode → Voice Isolation — filters other voices at the OS level, before audio reaches the page) is the single most effective lever — but it's manual and undetectable from the web. So `#micTips` is a one-time nudge (`showMicTips`/`closeMicTips`/`maybeAutoShowMicTips`): the iOS Voice-Isolation steps (shown when `isLikelyIOS()`, an Android note otherwise) plus the universal close-mic + push-to-talk-discipline habits. **Auto-shows once on the big-button surface** (in `applyBigButtonUI`), dismissal persists the additive per-device `micTipsSeen` field (no re-nag), and it's reopenable from the big-button top row and Options. The card now also explains *why* Voice Isolation/noise suppression don't stop other **voices** (they strip noise; speech survives) and points at the close-mic habit + the auto speaker filter. The heavier lever — **Scribe diarize + keep-primary-speaker** — is now **shipped** (default-on `#diarize`, see the STT section + `keepPrimarySpeaker`): it removes the second voice and reports what it dropped via a status note.
- **QR join**: the desktop renders a QR of `/?join=<code>` (in the pairing overlay, and a small one next to the code badge), generated by the embedded encoder (`qrMatrix` — byte mode, EC M, versions 1-6) — **never** an external QR image service, which would leak the code (the link's only credential). The phone's boot path (`restorePhoneLink`) consumes `?join=`, persists the join like a typed code, and scrubs the param from the address bar. Scenario 24 round-trips the rendered SVG through a real decoder (`jsqr`) — keep that test: a QR that renders but doesn't scan is a silent failure.
- **Pairing survives reloads**: `phoneSessionCode`, `joinedSessionCode`, and `lastDeliveryId` persist as additive `_v9` settings fields; `restorePhoneLink()` (boot, after `loadSettings`) resumes the desktop room / restores the phone's join, so an iOS PWA kill or tab reload cannot break the link. Persisting `lastDeliveryId` is what keeps the room's replay from double-copying across a reload. "End session" / "Leave" must clear the stored codes.
- **`GET /api/session/<code>/latest`** returns the room's newest held delivery (within retention) as `delivery` — back-compat for native pollers — plus the additive **`deliveries` array** (the full fresh ring, oldest→newest; the desktop's poll fallback sweeps it). `hotkey.ahk`'s optional phone-link poller uses `delivery` to write the clipboard with **no browser-focus requirement** (set `PHONE_POLL_URL` + `PHONE_CODE` at the top of the script). The poller baselines the first id it sees (never pastes a pre-existing delivery), dedupes by `delivery_id`, and skips polls while the PTT clipboard handshake is in flight (`BUSY`). Same trust model as the listener WS: the code is the only credential.
- **Big-button layout (joined devices)**: while `joinedSessionCode` is set (or `bigButtonMode` = "always" — a per-device additive `_v9` field; "never" wins over a join), `body.bigbtn` swaps the page for a fixed overlay: one center push-to-talk button + whole-screen status + transcript peek strip. The button has the hotkey's tap/hold semantics (`HOTKEY_TAP_MS`) and drives the normal `startRecording()`/`stopRecording()`/`pendingStart` paths — never parallel session logic. Input is pointer-events with capture; pointercancel/lostpointercapture/document-level release backstops mean a slide-away or multi-touch can never wedge the recording, and **a `pointercancel`/`lostpointercapture` stops the dictation regardless of hold duration** (the real release can never arrive — an open mic is never the right interpretation; a sub-tap-threshold cancel must NOT convert to toggle mode). The screen state (`updateBigScreen`) is **derived** from the existing `setStatus` class + mic/link pill transitions — the zero-listener "desktop link is DOWN" ack and relay failures land as `err` statuses *after* the local delivery, which is what reddens the screen (the queued next session waits on the relay ack and gives a failure ~1.5 s of screen time — see the session-state section); do not invent separate state for it. ## STT APIs (as used)

**One endpoint** — `POST /api/transcribe` → `handleTranscribeBatch`:

- Proxies to **ElevenLabs** `https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/speech-to-text` batch **`scribe_v2_medical`** with `xi-api-key` / `env.ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`. **Model = Scribe v2 Medical** (switched from `scribe_v2` 2026-09): ElevenLabs' clinical fine-tune of Scribe v2 (drug names, anatomy, pathology), same endpoint, same request shape, same price — every other field was deliberately left unchanged. The OpenAPI spec's `no_verbatim` description still says "Only supported with scribe_v2 model"; that line predates Medical — the 2026-09-11 changelog says keyterms, entity detection, diarization and no-verbatim "work the same way as Scribe v2", so don't "fix" `no_verbatim` off because of it. Scenario 49 pins the model id and the whole upstream request shape. Single-speaker medical config (`temperature=0`, `language_code=en`). **`diarize` is client-driven** and gated to the phone/big-button surface (`diarizeActive()`): `diarize=true` ⇒ word-granular, no forced `num_speakers`, client keeps the primary speaker (clear-majority guard + degraded-warn on big cuts); `diarize=false` (off, OR any plain-desktop dictation) ⇒ the proven `num_speakers=1`/`diarize=false`. Multipart fields: `api_key` or `passphrase` (shared mode injects the master key server-side after the constant-time `safeEqual`), `file` (webm/ogg from the `MediaRecorder`), `file_format=other`, `timestamps_granularity` (**always `word`** — the coverage guard needs per-word end times on every dictation; the Advanced select is retired/hidden), `no_verbatim`, `tag_audio_events`, `diarize`, `keyterms_json` (≤ 1000 terms, < 50 chars). Size gates 1 KB–25 MB; failures return a string `error` that surfaces in the loud failure status. The upload deadline is duration-aware (`batchUploadTimeoutMs`: 15 s floor + 25% of the take, capped +60 s).
- **Keep-primary-speaker (`keepPrimarySpeaker`, client).** When diarization is on, Scribe returns a `words[]` array with a `speaker_id` per token; bystander speech the mic caught lands as a *second* speaker. The client (in `batchTranscribe`) keeps only the dominant (most-word) speaker, reconstructs the text, and reports the dropped-word count. **It can only ever remove a second voice — never empty a clean note**: it no-ops (returns the unfiltered text) on a single speaker, no labels, or a missing/empty `words[]`. The Worker stays a thin proxy (it only shapes the request + passes the response through); the filtering is client-side so it's covered by the jsdom harness. Removals surface as a **no-beep status note** appended to the success line (the delivered text is already the cleaner, primary-only one — `opts.note` in `deliverFinalText`).
- **Keyterms** live in **`keyterms.js`** (`export const KEYTERM_PRESETS`, imported by `worker.js`), three-tier: `always:true` lists ride every dictation (pay the ~20 % surcharge — keep minimal), the rest are checkboxes (`presetIds` in `_v9` settings). Injected into the page via the `__KEYTERM_PRESETS__` token (**function replacer only**). Client merges via `effectiveKeyterms` (trim priority **custom > checked presets > always-on**), capped at **≤1000 terms / <50 chars** for batch. `sanitizeKeyterms` (server) + the client cap both apply.
- **Audio pipeline (batch):** `getUserMedia` → **makeup gain** (`micGainNode`) → high-pass → **look-ahead delay** (`lookaheadNode`) → **noise gate** → `destNode` → **`MediaRecorder.start(1000)`** webm/opus. The gate's analyser also drives the capture waveform + dead-mic watchdog. **The look-ahead delay is in the RECORDED branch ONLY** (`hpFilter → analyserNode` stays undelayed; `hpFilter → lookaheadNode → gateNode → destNode` is what gets recorded): the gate still decides from live audio, but gates audio `GATE_LOOKAHEAD_MS` (120) older, so the ~70 ms of detection lag (30 ms tick + ~21 ms window + 20 ms attack ramp) no longer eats the word onset — utterance-initial unvoiced consonants (f, s, h, p, t, th) sit below the 0.03 open threshold and were being attenuated *in the uploaded audio*. **Nothing that reads the analyser changes meaning** — the flatline watchdog, `probeMicAlive`, `maxRmsSeen`, the meter/waveform and the coverage guard's `lastGateOpenAtMs` baseline are all on the undelayed signal, which is what keeps every loud-failure guarantee intact. `createDelay` is feature-detected; without it the graph falls back to the exact previous wiring. Because the delay line always holds that much audio, **`stopRecording` defers `mediaRecorder.stop()` by `captureTailMs()` = look-ahead + `RELEASE_TAIL_MS` (80)** — stopping immediately would throw the delay line away and trade a clipped start for a clipped end. `stopping`/the "Stopping…" status are set first so the wait is never a visible hang; the deferred stop runs through `finishStopNow()` and `stopTailTimer` is cleared by `clearSessionTimers`. Both are per-device sliders and **0 disables either half**. (Scenario 45.) **Mic gain controls (for a too-quiet mic):** a per-device **`micGain`** makeup slider (1–20×, default 1) inserts `micGainNode` at the *head* of the graph (before the analyser), so it lifts a low-but-real signal above the flatline threshold + the gate for the recording AND the meter alike — but a **truly dead mic stays 0** (gain × 0 = 0), so it never masks a dead mic; the slider updates the node live (no rebuild). A per-device **`autoGain`** toggle (default OFF) flips the `autoGainControl` capture constraint (browser auto-boost; rebuilds the graph). **iOS quiet-mic level seed (foolproof out-of-the-box gain, `seedIosAudioDefaults`):** a quiet iPhone mic records below the gate and lands in the *silent dead-band* — RMS between `FLATLINE_RMS` (0.0008) and `gateOpen`, real-but-quiet speech that records near-empty *without* tripping the dead-mic alarm (this masqueraded as the "MIC NOT CAPTURING" failures the reliability work chased — see `REALTIME_HANDOFF.md` lineage). At boot (after `loadSettings`, before `tryWarmOnLoad`) an **additive, iOS-gated, one-shot** seed raises a **modest makeup gain (`IOS_SEED_MIC_GAIN` 3×, capped ≤4× to stay under the dead-mic-mask boundary)** and lowers the gate (`IOS_SEED_GATE_OPEN` 0.018, clear of `gateClose`) so a normal voice clears the gate *without anyone opening Advanced*. It's **deterministic + observable** (`gain:Nx` in `micDiag`) and a dead mic still reads 0 — chosen over browser AGC, which is opaque and can mask a dying mic / pump the gate open / make a bystander the kept speaker. Gated on `isLikelyIOS()` (never desktop), skipped on a hand-tuned device (`audioUserTuned`, stamped when `micGain`/`gateOpen`/`autoGain` change), and **versioned** (`audioSeedVersion` vs `IOS_AUDIO_SEED_VERSION`) so a corrected value can be re-pushed to un-tuned installs — both additive `_v9` fields, **no suffix bump**. **Loud failure by captured peak:** the empty/no-text finalize exits classify by `maxRmsSeen` so the loud failure names the real cause (all sentinel + one fail beep, disjoint from the flatline alarm, logged to the micfail ring): **peak < `FLATLINE_RMS`** ⇒ **"MIC PRODUCED NO SIGNAL"** (a dead / iOS-corpse capture — on a HELD take the 2.5 s watchdog already named it; on a SHORT press it slips past the watchdog, so name it here instead of blaming the clinician for "no speech"); **peak in `[FLATLINE_RMS, gateOpen)`** ⇒ **"VERY LOW MIC LEVEL"** (real but too quiet to clear the gate); **peak ≥ `gateOpen`** but empty ⇒ the genuine "no speech". So residual under-gain AND silent captures fail *loudly and named*, and the field diagnostic distinguishes low-gain from VPIO-silence. **Press-path corpse-mic probe (`probeMicAlive`, EVERY phone-surface press):** iOS can hand back a graph — REUSED or even a FRESH `getUserMedia` stream (the VPIO wall after an interruption; the echo-cancellation toggle did not fix it) — that reports "live"/unmuted but delivers pure silence after a *no-event* session reclaim (Low Power Mode / an idle gap), so the take would record nothing with no event to flag it. Every press on the big-button surface reads analyser frames until a live floor shows: a warm graph exits on frame 0 (~0 ms — the analyser continuously reflects the live mic), a fresh live graph within a frame or two while its first buffers fill (not capturable audio anyway, so the wait can never swallow speech), and only a dead mic pays the `MIC_PROBE_SETTLE_MS` (400 ms) bound. A dead read forces ONE fresh `getUserMedia` + re-probe; a mic **still dead then fails LOUD *before capture*** (sentinel + failBeep + "MIC NOT CAPTURING … recording did NOT start", logged as `precapture-dead-mic`) — REC never starts on a provably silent mic, so a clinician cannot dictate a whole take into a corpse and lose it to the watchdog after the fact. Fully best-effort (any probe failure lets the press proceed; the watchdog stays the backstop). **Idle mic-health sampler (`micIdleSample`, big-button surface):** between takes iOS can kill the retained mic with NO event — one analyser frame every `MIC_IDLE_PROBE_MS` (4 s) while idle + visible catches the corpse early: `MIC_IDLE_DEAD_COUNT` (2) consecutive dead frames ⇒ proactive rebuild (free while nobody speaks) + the top-row **`#bigMicPill`** ("Mic ✓" / "Mic ⚠ rebuilding"). A cue + heal only — never runs mid-session, never load-bearing. `noiseSuppression` **DEFAULTS ON** — there's an Options A/B toggle (modern batch STT sometimes does better with it OFF on a close mic; per-device, untested). `echoCancellation` is **also a per-device toggle (`echoCancel`, defaults ON** = unchanged behavior): on iOS, EC runs capture through the **voice-processing audio unit**, which is the path most prone to handing the Web Audio graph **silence after a session interruption** (call/Siri/lock) even on the built-in mic — the suspected cause of the persistent "MIC NOT CAPTURING" / silent-capture failures that survive the corpse-mic rebuild; turning EC **off** uses the plain mic path. Changing either toggle rebuilds the graph (`releaseAudio`→`tryWarmOnLoad`). On release, `recorder.onstop` flushes the last <1 s into `chunks[]` and `finishBatchSession` uploads. **Silent-capture diagnostic:** the MIC-FAIL status appends the live audio-graph state — `[ctx:<state> ctxSR:<rate> micSR:<rate> rs:<readyState> mute:<bool> dev:<mic label> ec:<on|off> agc:<on|off> gain:<N>x peak:<maxRms>]` — so a failure reads as a precise bug report (Bluetooth/HFP route, sample-rate mismatch, or EC/session silence) instead of a generic message; it's mirrored onto the phone big-screen `#bigStatus`.
- **Finalize/upload latency optimizations (`[LATENCY]`-tagged).** (1) `MediaRecorder.start(1000)` timeslice — chunks accrue *during* recording so `onstop` flushes <1 s, not the whole take (don't drop the arg). (2) **`precomputedBatchKeyterms`** snapshots the `effectiveKeyterms()` merge/dedup at session *start*, off the stop→upload path. (3) A **TLS pre-warm** (`new Image().src="/favicon.ico?warm=…"` at session start) opens TCP/TLS before the upload — deliberately an `Image`, not `fetch()`, so it never consumes a batch queue slot or trips the harness's queue-driven `fetch` mock. (4) The **streamed upload** (see the hard invariant) — the audio is already at ElevenLabs when the key comes up.

## Latency instrumentation + the window recording cue

**Latency instrumentation (`[PERF]`, Phase 0 of `PERF_PLAN.md`).** Every finalized take — success *and* loud failure — records one entry in the per-device `scribe_v2_timing_v9` ring (cap `TIMING_LOG_MAX`=50, additive, never wiped by a settings change). Stage stamps (`takeTimings`, `stampTake`, first-write-wins so a retried finalize cannot move a stage backwards): `release` (`stopRecording`) → `onstop` (`finalizeSession`) → `uploadStart`/`headers`/`body` (`batchTranscribe`) → `delivered` (`deliverFinalText`, the single delivery exit, so exactly one entry per take). The Worker adds a **`Server-Timing`** header (`parse` = buffering/re-serializing the upload, `el` = the ElevenLabs round trip, `worker` = total in-Worker), which the client folds in — so **`net` = `service` − `worker`** is our own network overhead, the only part transport work could ever recover. **Durations only: never log the request URL or body in the Worker** (both carry the passphrase). **A FAILED take is no longer a row of blanks** (2026-09): the entry also carries `stage` (the furthest stamp that fired — `uploadStart` means the response never came back, `headers` means the service answered and the body read died), `errKind` (`timeout` / `network` / `http-<status>`) and `deadline` (the budget it blew), and the readout leads with those instead of the empty timing line. That is what distinguishes "our upload leg ate the deadline" from "ElevenLabs was slow" without guessing — read it against the ElevenLabs request log. The readout's second line is the **verdict summary** over the whole ring (`timingSummaryLine`): how many takes failed, how many got **no reply from the service at all** (stage never reached `headers`), and the median ElevenLabs vs median our-network times — so "is the lag ElevenLabs or us?" is one glance, not a spreadsheet export. Each row also names its **`path`** (`stream` / `batch` / `stream>batch`) and a one-line **`pathNote`** (why a stream fell back, or that streaming is off until reload); on the stream path `Server-Timing` is measured from the end frame (`el`/`worker` = the wait after the release, `open` = how long the stream ran), so the numbers compare directly with the batch path's. Classes and durations only, never a URL or body. Surfaced as a readout + **Copy timing log** (TSV) / **Clear timing log** in Advanced, and `?perf=1` also console-logs each take. Wholly additive and try/caught throughout — instrumentation must never affect a delivery. (Scenario 43.)

**Window-wide recording cue (`windowTint`, per-device).** The app is used in a PWA window shrunk to a sliver beside Cerner on machines with **no speakers**, where the in-card REC pill and waveform are invisible exactly when they matter. `applyWindowState()` mirrors the state into `body[data-state]` (whole-window colour wash, or an edge frame via the fixed `#stateEdge` overlay), the tab **title**, the **favicon** (an inline-SVG state disc), and — feature-detected, best-effort, never the only cue — `navigator.setAppBadge` and `theme-color`. **No new state**: both this and the phone big screen read the extracted `currentUiState()`, and the CSS is `:not(.bigbtn)` so the phone overlay (which owns its own screen state) is untouched. Modes: `full` (default) / `border` / `off`. (Scenario 44.)

**AutoHotkey focus return (`hotkey.ahk`).** A clipboard write needs document focus, so the script activates the dictation window and must hold it until the browser writes — which used to leave the clinician *in* the dictation window, ending every take with an alt-tab back to Cerner (1–3 s, larger than anything in the transcription pipeline). It now captures `PREV_HWND := WinActive("A")` **before** stealing focus and calls `ReturnFocus()` **after** `ClipWait`, never before. `AUTO_PASTE` is **off by default** and fires only when focus was restored to the exact captured handle and the result was not the sentinel — the handle being right does not prove the *caret* is, and a paste into the wrong field is a wrong-chart risk. A `WS_EX_NOACTIVATE` + `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` always-on-top state bar (RECORDING → TRANSCRIBING → READY/FAILED) is the cue that still works when the PWA window is fully covered; showing it can never pull focus off Cerner. `MIN_HOLD` dropped 350→200 ms — short utterances ("yes", "negative") finish inside 350 ms and the old value silently refused to paste real takes.

When changing the session flow, beeps, clipboard behavior, engines, or watchdog: **update/extend `tests/flow.test.mjs` scenarios in the same change** and run them. The harness mocks `WebSocket`, `fetch` (queue-driven; an empty queue answers 500 so unexpected uploads fail loudly), `AudioContext`, `getUserMedia`, `MediaRecorder` (delivers a real Blob on stop), the clipboard, and — for the dictation journal — `indexedDB` (via `fake-indexeddb`, wired per-DOM in the journal/lever scenarios; the main DOM has none, so the journal cleanly self-disables there).

jsdom gotchas baked into the harness: define `window.isSecureContext = true` and stub `navigator.clipboard`, or every clipboard path "fails"; stub `URL.createObjectURL` (the preview path runs whenever the recorder delivers chunks); the mock `MediaRecorder` must deliver a real `Blob` via `ondataavailable` on `stop()` so the batch upload has a body; let the gate-meter loop tick (a short `sleep`) for the dead-mic watchdog + the capture-feedback show/hide to fire. **`npm install --no-save` prunes other `--no-save` packages — install `jsdom jsqr fake-indexeddb` in ONE command.** The dictation journal stores chunks as **`ArrayBuffer`s** (not Blobs): `fake-indexeddb`'s structured clone mangles jsdom Blobs, and jsdom `FormData` rejects Node Blobs — `ArrayBuffer` round-trips through both (and is the safer real-world choice for iOS Safari's Blob-in-IDB bugs). The journal scenario shares ONE `IDBFactory` across the "crash" and "reboot" DOMs to simulate persistence across a process death; a periodic-`ondataavailable` `MediaRecorder` mock is needed so chunks actually land mid-recording.

## Deployment

**Deploy = push to `main`** — Cloudflare **Workers Builds auto-deploys** on push (worker name `eleven` — the pre-merge batch app's URL, so users' localStorage survives; secrets persist across deploys). Confirm via `gh api repos/praxeo/elevenlabs-web/commits/<sha>/check-runs` (the `Workers Builds: eleven` check). `npx wrangler deploy` also works but the local OAuth token is **scope-limited**: it CANNOT write secrets (`auth error 10000`) — **set secrets via the Cloudflare dashboard** (Workers & Pages → eleven → Settings). Secrets in use: **`ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`** (the batch transcription) and **`APP_PASSPHRASE`** (gates shared mode). `SONIOX_API_KEY` and the Deepgram/AI-Gateway/`PROBE_KEY` secrets are no longer used (realtime removed) and can be deleted from the dashboard. **Bindings** (`wrangler.toml`, provisioned automatically on deploy — not secrets): the `SESSION_ROOM` Durable Object and the `[[ratelimits]]` `SESSION_RL`/`SESSION_FAIL_RL` rate limiters (the Worker fail-opens if a plan lacks the rate-limit binding, so the deploy still succeeds). HTML served `no-store`. Note (workflow on the feature branch in this repo): pushes to the working branch ALSO build/deploy to the live worker, so a broken push goes live — validate before pushing.

## Known sharp edges

- Clipboard writes require document focus; both `navigator.clipboard` and the `execCommand` fallback fail unfocused. The UX (beeps, sentinel) is built around that constraint — don't "fix" it by silently retrying later. (The phone-link focus-retry is the one sanctioned exception: it is loud while pending and retries only on refocus — see the Phone link section.)
- The shared-mode passphrase travels as `?auth=` on the phone-link WS upgrade URL (browsers cannot set WS headers; every other session call sends the `x-phone-auth` header instead); **don't add logging of request URLs in the Worker**, and know that enabling Workers observability/logpush would capture upgrade URLs. The batch POST carries the passphrase in the form body; the streamed upload (`/api/transcribe-stream`) carries it in the `x-app-auth` header (a BYO key in `x-el-key`) — never log request headers either.
- "Copy & clear" (`copyBtn`) and "Clear dictation box" (`freshBtn`) are now **disabled and guarded during the in-flight upload** (`recording || stopping || finishing`), closing the old collision where a box cleared mid-upload reappeared with the delivered note (the delivery rebuilds `finalizedSegments` from `sessionBaseText`). "Clear history" (`clearBtn`) is unaffected (it mutates history, not the box).
- The audio preview/download is the **post-gate** recording — i.e. exactly what was uploaded/transcribed (the gate is load-bearing in batch), so an over-aggressive gate that clips the preview also clipped the transcript. Tune the gate, not just the preview.
- **Phone link in batch:** the phone has no live text, so the desktop's `remoteCommitted`/`phone_session_end` grace-fallback never fires — the desktop relies entirely on the authoritative `phone_delivery` POST. That delivery is acked + replayed + deduped, and a phone that dies *before* delivering is covered by the **phone-side delivery queue** (post-transcription leg) and a phone that dies *mid-recording* (audio not yet uploaded) is now covered by the **dictation journal** (pre-transcription leg — see below). The one gap the journal can't close: a dead mic that captured no real audio (the journal recovers a crash, not a silent mic — that's the watchdog's job).
- **Dictation journal (crash-safe audio recovery — closes the last silent loss window).** During recording, each `MediaRecorder` chunk is mirrored to **IndexedDB** (`scribe_v2_journal`, an `ArrayBuffer` per chunk — *not* a Blob: iOS Safari has Blob-in-IDB bugs) keyed to a per-take `sessions` record (`state:"recording"`). The single delivery exit (`deliverFinalText`) clears the record once the take reaches its cued outcome — **except on a failed/timed-out upload**, where it calls `journalDetach()` to KEEP the audio for a retry (see the failed-upload invariant above) — so a take that **never finalized** (crash / OS kill / OOM before/during upload) or whose **upload failed** is left recoverable. Boot (`restoreJournal`, after `restoreLatestFromHistory`) finds the newest orphan, rebuilds the audio Blob, and shows the loud `#journalRecover` banner (warn beep) with **Recover** (re-upload via `batchTranscribe` → deliver locally, never auto-relayed to a stale desktop link → clear) and **Discard**. **Purely additive + best-effort**: every IndexedDB op is try/caught and no-ops if IDB is missing/fails (`journalDisabled`), so the journal can only ever *narrow* the loss window, never widen or block the live capture/upload path. Older orphans are dropped (a stale multi-hour recovery must never auto-land on a chart). The mirror caps at `JOURNAL_MAX_CHUNKS` (1800 ≈ 30 min); a recovery that hit the cap **says so in the banner** ("only about the first 30 minutes…") instead of silently offering a truncated note. (Scenarios 32j, 41.)
